About Us

Cantonese cooking with a generous Melbourne welcome

Golden Dreams brings the rhythm of a Hong Kong dining room to Southbank: bamboo steamers arriving hot from the kitchen, seafood finished with ginger and aged soy, and roast dishes carved for the table.

Lunch is relaxed and lively, dinner is slower and more ceremonial, and every service is built around sharing food with people you want to stay at the table with.

  • Handmade dim sum prepared fresh each day
  • Seasonal Australian seafood cooked in Cantonese style
  • Private banquet seating available for families and groups
